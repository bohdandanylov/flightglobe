Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Overview

FlightGlobe (“we”, “us”) is a travel journal app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and where it is stored.

FlightGlobe is designed to keep your travel journal on your device. We do not operate a FlightGlobe user account server that stores your profile or flights in our cloud.

Information on Your Device

Depending on how you use the app, FlightGlobe may store on your device profile details you enter such as full name, citizenship, and email; your travel journal, including saved flights, tracked flights, visited and wishlist countries, world wonders, and country-day tracking; app preferences such as distance and temperature units; and widget summary statistics shared with the FlightGlobe widget through an App Group on your device.

Profile and sign-in related details may be stored in the device Keychain (This Device Only). Travel journal data is stored locally using on-device storage.

Location

With your permission, FlightGlobe may access your device location to center the map on your current position, automatically suggest the country for day tracking, and request local weather for your current area.

Location is used only to provide these features. We do not sell your location data. You can deny or change location access in iOS Settings. Some features will not work without location permission.

Information Sent to Third Parties

To provide core features, FlightGlobe may send limited data to third-party services. AeroDataBox (via RapidAPI) receives flight number and date information needed to look up or refresh flight status. Open-Meteo receives approximate coordinates to retrieve current weather. Apple services such as MapKit and reverse geocoding are used for map display and converting a location into a country.

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. FlightGlobe does not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell your personal information.

Sharing You Choose

If you use share features (for example sharing passport content to Instagram Stories or other apps), content you choose to share is sent to those apps under their terms and privacy policies.

Analytics and Tracking

FlightGlobe does not use third-party analytics SDKs for advertising profiling and does not use App Tracking Transparency / IDFA advertising tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your profile and travel journal are stored on your device. Saved flights remain on the device until you delete them or use Delete Profile. Delete Profile removes your profile and all related travel data from this device. Uninstalling FlightGlobe also removes local app data stored on that device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of FlightGlobe after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data in FlightGlobe can be sent through Contact Support in the app.

This Privacy Policy is provided for transparency about how FlightGlobe works. It is not legal advice.
