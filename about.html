About the app

FlightGlobe is a travel journal for iPhone — flights, maps, passport context, and a globe that makes your routes feel real.

What you can do

Log flights and watch them across an interactive globe. Browse statistics for distance, time in the air, aircraft, and delays. Explore visited countries, wishlist destinations, and world wonders. Check passport ranking and visa-oriented maps. Track selected flights and glance at local weather when you allow location.

Designed to stay personal

Your profile and travel journal are stored on your device. FlightGlobe does not run a user-account cloud for your flights. Optional lookups use third-party services for flight status, maps, and weather — always for the feature you asked for.

Who builds it

FlightGlobe is made by Bohdan Danylov, founder and engineer. The app is intended for personal convenience and travel record-keeping — not as an airline, ticket agency, or official source of immigration or flight-status advice.

Get the app

FlightGlobe is available for iPhone. Search for FlightGlobe on the App Store, or open the listing from your device once published.
