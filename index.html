A quieter way to remember travel

FlightGlobe is built for people who fly and want the record to feel cinematic — not like another spreadsheet of boarding passes.

  • Globe & routes

    See your flights arc across Apple Maps in 3D or flat view, with airports and visited countries on the map.

  • Passport story

    Follow visa insights, rankings, border stamps, and world wonders as your personal travel passport grows.

  • On your device

    Your journal stays local. Look up flights, track status, and keep stats without a FlightGlobe cloud account.
